Individual Life Insurance Policy

ABC Life and Annuity Company agrees to provide the benefits described in this policy while the policy is in force, subject to all provisions, exclusions, riders, and payment requirements stated below.

Right to Examine Policy

The Owner may return this policy within 30 days after receipt. If the policy is returned during this period, ABC Life and Annuity Company will refund all premiums paid and the policy will be considered void from the Policy Date.

Definitions

Company means ABC Life and Annuity Company. Owner means the person who holds all ownership rights under this policy. Insured means the person whose life is insured by this policy. Beneficiary means the person or entity entitled to receive the death benefit.

Insuring Agreement

Subject to the terms of this policy, the Company will pay the death benefit to the Beneficiary when satisfactory proof is received that the Insured died while this policy was in force.

Premium Provisions

Premiums are due monthly on the 15th day of each month. The planned monthly premium is $97.50. Premiums may be paid by electronic bank draft, check, or another method approved by the Company.

This policy has a 31-day grace period. If a premium is not paid by the end of the grace period, the policy will lapse without value unless reinstated under the reinstatement provision.

Death Benefit

The death benefit is the face amount shown on the Policy Schedule, less any unpaid premium due through the month of death. Payment will be made in one sum unless a settlement option is elected.

Beneficiary Provisions

The Owner may change the Beneficiary by written request while the Insured is living. A beneficiary change takes effect when recorded by the Company, subject to any payment made before recording.

Exclusions and Limitations

If the Insured dies by suicide, while sane or insane, within two years from the Policy Date, the Company's liability will be limited to the premiums paid, less any policy debt.

Accelerated Death Benefit Rider

This rider may allow a portion of the death benefit to be paid before death if the Insured is diagnosed with a qualifying terminal illness. Any accelerated amount will reduce the death benefit payable at death.

Execution

This policy is issued by ABC Life and Annuity Company at its home office in Springfield, Illinois.
